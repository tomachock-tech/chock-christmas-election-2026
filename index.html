Candidates Declare Victory Over the Concept of Counting

Max delivered the Grinch’s victory speech by sitting on it. Jack conceded Halloween and demanded a recount of Christmas. Marley repossessed his own podium halfway through Scrooge’s remarks. The Mayor has endorsed both faces and is polling third among them.

The Commission has reviewed its review of the review. Polls still close at 4 p.m. Hawaiʻi time. We must preserve the APPEARANCE of choice and fairness. The Commission is pleased to report that the word “appearance” is holding up beautifully.

All Three Campaigns Withdraw Their Endorsements of Each Other

None had issued any. Max accused Jack of wearing a wire. It was his bow tie. Marley demanded the bow tie be seized as collateral. The Mayor’s two faces held rival press conferences from the same podium and accused each other of leaking the seating chart.

Election Commission statement
Tonight’s emergency meeting has been moved to an undisclosed location. This is because nobody wrote down where it was.

The Elf Papers · Sat, Nov. 14 · 6:00 AM HST

Leaked Memos Put Same Elf on Both Lists

An anonymous elf delivered two memos proving he was Naughty and Nice, respectively. Max declared the second memo a forgery. Marley confirmed both documents were authentic and billed the elf twice. Jack’s campaign named him its director of bipartisan outreach.

Election Commission statement
An independent investigator has cleared the Commission. The Commission thanks itself for making time on short notice.

Evening Extra · Fri, Nov. 13 · 6:00 PM HST

Jack Announces $2 Billion Christmas Upgrade

The Mayor of Halloween Town unveiled plans for pumpkin-powered sleighs and a gift-delivery tunnel under December. His other face said the $2 billion was a typo. Jack clarified that it was a down payment. Scrooge asked who was underwriting December.

Election Commission statement
No portion of the family’s $50 has been pledged to the tunnel. The Commission regrets having to use the word “portion.”

Morning Edition · Fri, Nov. 13 · 6:00 AM HST

Grinch Exposes Christmas-Industrial Complex

Max released a chart connecting Scrooge to Big Ribbon and Jack to a suspiciously well-funded caroling lobby. The chart was held together with gift tape. Marley called the allegation defamatory and asked whether the tape was purchased at retail.

Election Commission statement
The Commission investigated itself and found no wrongdoing. It also found the tape, which it will be keeping.

Opening Hostilities · Thu, Nov. 12 · 6:00 PM HST

Scrooge Demands Audit of Fifty Dollars

Jacob Marley filed a request for receipts, ribbon inventories, and proof that each gift bag has been depreciated correctly. Max, speaking for the Grinch campaign, submitted one chewed receipt and declined to identify the merchant.

Election Commission statement
The $50 budget remains $50. The Commission has opened an inquiry into why this required a press conference.

Election rules

  • Pick which draw we use. A, B, and C each represent a prepared Secret Santa draw. Nine people take part, eight vote, and Dad serves as Election Commissioner and sits out the vote. A tie for first goes to a random draw from a lauhala basket. Just like we do in Hawaii. Because drawing out of a lauhala basket is pure democracy in action. Ahem.
  • Shop for one person with a $50 total budget. One $50 gift, a $30 gift and a $20 gift, or five $10 surprises all work. Everyone gets the same shopping budget regardless of what they contribute. Mom handles contributions privately.
  • Watch for the result Sunday night. After announcing the winner, Dad will text each person privately with whom they’re shopping for.
  • Add gift ideas to the shared Google spreadsheet. Look for the link in the family text thread.
